This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how auth gcalendar (“the App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information, including data obtained through Google services. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. General information

The data controller for data processed in connection with the App is Ignatov Sergei, Russia (“Controller”). For any questions about this Policy or data processing, contact us at sergey.ignatov@1win.pro.

This Policy applies to the App's website hosted on GitHub Pages and to the App itself. It does not apply to third-party websites or services, even if the App links to them; their data practices are governed by their own policies.

2. Data we process

Depending on the features you use, we may process the following categories of data:

  • Google account data. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, profile picture and unique Google account identifier. The scope of data is determined by the permissions you confirm on the Google consent screen.
  • Google Calendar data. If you grant access to Google Calendar, we receive the list of your calendars and event details such as titles, dates, times, descriptions and other fields needed to display and work with events. The exact scope of access is determined by the permissions on the Google consent screen.
  • Data you provide. The content of your requests, text, files, settings and other information you enter or upload while using the App's features.
  • Technical data. Device and browser information, IP address, date and time of access, error logs and security events — to the extent necessary to operate, diagnose and protect the App.
  • Usage data. De-identified information about which features are used and how often, where necessary to improve the App.

We do not request or attempt to access data that is not needed for the stated features. We do not collect payment card data unless the App offers a corresponding payment feature.

3. Google user data and Limited Use

The App uses Google APIs, including Google Sign-In and the Google Calendar API. Access to Calendar is granted only with your explicit permission, and the specific permissions requested are shown on the Google consent screen before access is granted.

With respect to information received from Google APIs, we commit to:

  • using it only to provide and improve the features described in this Policy;
  • not selling it or transferring it to data brokers, advertising networks or other third parties for advertising purposes;
  • not using it to serve personalized ads, including retargeting;
  • not using it for purposes incompatible with the purposes for which it was obtained;
  • granting employee access only to the extent necessary for support and security of the App.

Limited Use. auth gcalendar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Human access to Google user data is permitted only in the following cases: you have given explicit consent; it is necessary for security or to prevent abuse; it is required by law; or the data is aggregated and used for internal operations, provided that it is de-identified.

You can review and revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time on the Third-party apps with account access page. After revocation, the App can no longer access Google data, but data previously stored may remain with us until the retention period expires or until you request deletion.

4. How we use data

We process data to:

  • provide the App's features and enable account sign-in;
  • display and work with Google Calendar events within the permissions granted;
  • save your settings and work results;
  • respond to support requests and resolve issues;
  • ensure security, prevent fraud and abuse;
  • analyze App performance in de-identified form and improve it;
  • comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not use your data to make decisions that produce legal effects concerning you without your involvement. We do not sell personal data.

5. Legal bases

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we process personal data on the following bases:

  • your consent — for example, for access to Google data and optional communications;
  • performance of a contract — providing the App features you use;
  • legitimate interests — security, abuse prevention and improvement of the App, provided your rights do not override them;
  • legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with the law.

You may withdraw consent at any time; this does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

6. Sharing data

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data may be shared with:

  • infrastructure and hosting providers that process data on our behalf under agreements requiring confidentiality;
  • Google — as part of using Google services and subject to Google's own terms;
  • public authorities — where required by law, court order or a valid request;
  • a successor — in the event of a reorganization, merger or sale of the business, with continued data protection.

We do not share Google data with third parties for advertising, third-party analytics or training third-party models, except where expressly authorized by you or required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

We retain data no longer than necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or for a period required by law. De-identified security logs may be retained for up to 12 months. Account data is typically deleted or de-identified within 30 days after account deletion or receipt of a deletion request, unless the law requires longer retention.

You can delete your data in the following ways:

  • revoke access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions;
  • delete data inside the App, if such a feature is available;
  • send a request to sergey.ignatov@1win.pro from the email associated with your account, specifying which data should be deleted.

We may ask you to verify your identity to protect your account. Requests are handled within 30 days; if more time is needed, we will let you know.

8. Security

We use organizational and technical safeguards, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), restricted access for employees and providers, security event logging and regular updates of components. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

The App's website on GitHub Pages does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not embed third-party trackers. The App itself may use strictly necessary cookies and browser local storage for sign-in, saving settings and maintaining session security. Disabling such cookies may make some features unavailable.

GitHub Pages hosting may process technical data (such as IP addresses) in accordance with the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. International transfers

We and our service providers may process data in countries other than your country of residence. When transferring data to such countries, we apply legally recognized safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where required.

11. Children
